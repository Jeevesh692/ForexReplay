# 0026: Gold (XAUUSD)

- **Date:** 25 Oct 2026
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decided by:** Jeevesh Prakash ("add XAUUSD data too"; he exported the files from MT5 himself), with Claude on the lot size, the pip and what to leave out

## The data

1-minute candles from his FundedNext account, 31 Jul 2025 to 22 Oct 2026: 435,592 minutes, kept as `data/XAUUSD_M1_mt5_2025-07_2026-10.csv.gz` (6.6 MB). Checked: 0 broken candles. Of the 251 gaps of 30 minutes to 6 hours, 248 end at 18:00 New York time and most last 61 minutes. That is gold's daily break, so the server clock is the same New York + 7 hours as the currency pairs.

**No older history.** His H1, H4 and D1 exports start on 27 Oct 2025, after the minutes begin, so they add nothing the minutes do not already give, and they were not added. So gold has no 1-hour swing mode yet ([0025](0025-replay-on-hourly-candles.md)). M5, M15 and M30 were not needed: the app builds them from the minutes.

## Money: a lot of gold is 100 ounces

A currency-pair lot is 100,000 units; a gold lot is **100 ounces**. The data now carries a `contract_size` per symbol (100,000 unless stated), and `pointValueFor` uses it. Gold has 2 digits, so a point is $0.01, and a point on one lot is 100 × $0.01 = **$1**.

- **Assumed, not yet confirmed:** 100 ounces is the usual figure, but it was not read from FundedNext's symbol specification. If MT5's Properties for XAUUSD shows another contract size, change `contract_size` in `forex_replay/datapipe.py` and rebuild.
- **A pip on gold is taken as $0.10** (10 points), the common convention. Pips are only used for display and the ADR plan; the engine works in points and R, so the choice changes no fill or result.

Without the lot size, sizing would have used 100,000 units and been 1,000 times wrong. A test holds it: 1% of $10,000 with a $10 stop gives 0.10 lots.
